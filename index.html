About Project

The Mini CRM helps businesses organize and manage client leads effectively through a clean and user-friendly interface.

The Mini CRM is designed to help businesses manage leads generated from website contact forms. Users can view lead information, update lead statuses, add notes, schedule follow-ups and maintain customer relationships effectively.

The system improves lead tracking and ensures no potential customer opportunity is missed. It also provides a better workflow for managing sales pipelines and customer communication.

Key Features

The CRM system provides all essential functionalities required to manage leads and customer interactions smoothly.

📋 Lead Listing

Store client name, email, source and status.

🔄 Status Updates

Update status as New, Contacted or Converted.

📝 Follow-Ups

Add notes and reminders for each lead.

🔐 Admin Login

Secure administrator access for CRM data.

⚡ CRUD Operations

Create, Read, Update and Delete leads.

📊 Analytics

Track lead conversion performance.
